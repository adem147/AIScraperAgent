Phase 2 Architecture Roadmap

Key extensions and upcoming developments for the automated intelligence system.

01

Progressive PDF Parser

Automated crawler to download and parse attached tender PDFs (terms of reference, tender specifications) progressively.

02

NLP Key-Field Extractor

Fine-tuned Named Entity Recognition (NER) & LLM pipeline to extract key dates, estimated budget, eligibility criteria, and required documents.

03

Multilingual Processing

Cross-lingual vector embeddings and automated translation supporting French, English, and Arabic (FR / EN / AR).

04

Automated Alert Engine

FastAPI subscription webhooks & email notifications whenever a high-scoring tender (>0.85 similarity) matches registered CERT profiles.
